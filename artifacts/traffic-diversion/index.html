PROJECT NOTES · HYDERABAD

RouteWise, in plain terms.

A student demonstration of how predicting congestion can help choose a better route when roads get busy.

RWDEMO
MODEL
01 / PURPOSE

What does the app do?

RouteWise compares route choices across a simplified Hyderabad road network. The live route screen lets you choose a start and destination, explore estimated congestion, and see alternate routes. The simulation screen runs two approaches side by side so their travel times can be compared.

Important: congestion and vehicle movement are simulated for this demo. They are not live traffic data.

02 / DECISION FLOW

Prediction → route choice → diversion

  1. Predict: estimate how busy road segments may become from the selected scenario.
  2. Choose: compare the predicted travel cost of possible paths.
  3. Divert: recommend a less delayed path when its expected journey is better.
03 / INCIDENT MODE

What does the circle mean?

Turn on incident mode, then click the map to mark or move the disruption point. The radius is the affected-road distance around that point. Segments inside it receive extra delay and a red dashed treatment.

The radius is not the delay amount; it describes how far the disruption reaches.

05 / LIMITS

A demo, not a navigation service.

The road network and traffic behavior are simplified, and the model does not receive live sensors, incidents, or driver reports. Real-world performance would need current data, stronger prediction validation, and testing with actual road conditions.
